# Azul: rules summary (team paraphrase used as the ingestion corpus)

This is our own plain-language summary of the Azul rules, written for the rule-extraction experiments. It is not the publisher's rulebook.

## Components

Azul uses tiles in five colours: blue, yellow, red, black and white. There are 100 tiles in total, 20 of each colour, kept in a bag. Each player has a personal board with three areas: five pattern lines on the left, a wall of 25 squares on the right, and a floor line of seven spaces along the bottom. A round token marks who starts the next round.

## Setup

The number of factory displays depends on how many people play. With 2 players the table uses 5 factory displays. With 3 players it uses 7 factory displays. With 4 players it uses 9 factory displays. At the start of every round, four tiles are drawn at random from the bag and placed on each factory display. When the bag runs out, the discarded tiles are returned to it.

## Taking turns

On your turn you must take tiles in one of two ways. You can pick one factory display, choose a colour from it, and take every tile of that colour; the tiles of other colours left on that display slide into the centre of the table. Or you can choose a colour in the centre and take all tiles of that colour from there. The first player to take from the centre in a round also takes the starting token and must put it on the first space of their floor line, where it counts like a tile and costs points; that player begins the next round.

## Placing tiles

The tiles you took must all go onto a single pattern line. The pattern lines hold 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 tiles, from the top line to the bottom line. A line can only contain one colour, and you cannot use a line if that colour is already on the wall in the same row. Tiles that do not fit are dropped on the floor line, and you may also choose to put all of them there.

## The floor line

Each tile on the floor line costs points at the end of the round. Reading the seven spaces from left to right, the penalties are: -1, -1, -2, -2, -2, -3, -3. Extra tiles beyond the seventh space are simply discarded with no further penalty.

## Wall tiling and scoring

When everyone has finished picking, every full pattern line moves exactly one tile onto the matching square of the wall in its row; the other tiles of that line are discarded. The first row of the wall is arranged blue, yellow, red, black, white from left to right. Each row below is the row above shifted one place to the right, wrapping around, so that no colour repeats in a row or column.

A newly placed tile scores by its neighbours. Count the connected horizontal chain of tiles that includes it and the connected vertical chain that includes it, and add the sizes of the chains longer than one tile. A tile with no neighbours scores 1 point.

## End of the game

The game finishes at the end of the round in which at least one player has completed a horizontal row of five tiles on their wall. After the last round, bonus points are added: 2 points for each completed horizontal row, 7 points for each completed vertical column, and 10 points for each colour that has all five of its tiles on the wall. The player with the most points wins.
